# Citation validation note

Citation ID validity answers only whether an identifier resolves to a stored EvidenceItem. It does not prove that the evidence entails the sentence that cites it. A paragraph about market growth could cite an existing checkpointing EvidenceItem and still be semantically unsupported.

Citation correctness compares each claim with its linked evidence. Citation completeness checks whether all externally verifiable claims have support. A trustworthy report needs all three checks: validity, correctness, and completeness.
